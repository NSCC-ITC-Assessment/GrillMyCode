## Where your questions come from

1. Your instructor added GrillMyCode to this assignment.
2. When triggered (via push or tag, or manually by your instructor), GrillMyCode reads what you changed and writes questions about it.
3. A few minutes later, it posts them as an issue in your repository on GitHub.
4. GrillMyCode Companion reads that issue and shows the questions in the GrillMyCode view, beside your code.

The extension doesn't write the questions. While you are working in VS Code, it looks for new ones about once a minute. Your first set appears in the list as soon as it is found. When a later set replaces it, a notification says so, and the list changes when you select **Show New Questions** there or **Refresh Questions** at the top of the list.

No questions yet? Check that you have pushed your work, and its tag if your instructor asked for one. Wait a few minutes, or select **Refresh Questions** to look now. The same questions are always in the **Issues** tab of your repository on GitHub.
